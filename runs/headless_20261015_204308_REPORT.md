## What is this?

80 tracking scenarios were run automatically. Each scenario gets a score and a verdict:

- **lock%** = how often the tracker held the target. Higher is better.
- **rmse** = average position error in pixels. Lower is better.
- **losses** = how many times the tracker lost the target and had to re-find it. Lower is better.
- **1st** = first frame where it locked on. Lower / earlier is better.
- **STRICT-PASS** = beat all targets. **INTERMITTENT-PASS** = passed under the easier blinking-target rule. **FAIL** = missed at least one target. **ERROR** = crashed (none here).
